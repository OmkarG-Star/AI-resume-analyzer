Machine Learning Engineer

About the role
Our ML platform team ships the models behind pricing, search ranking and demand forecasting. You will take models from notebook to production and keep them healthy.

What you will do
- Package and deploy models as low-latency services
- Build training pipelines with experiment tracking and automated evaluation
- Monitor drift and model quality in production and lead retraining
- Work with data engineers on feature pipelines

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience building machine learning systems
- Strong Python and SQL
- PyTorch or TensorFlow, plus Scikit-learn
- Model deployment with FastAPI or Flask and Docker
- MLOps practices: MLflow, CI/CD and Git
- Bachelor's degree in computer science or a related field

Nice to have
- Kubernetes and AWS or GCP
- Experience with NLP or LLMs
- Spark for large-scale feature engineering
