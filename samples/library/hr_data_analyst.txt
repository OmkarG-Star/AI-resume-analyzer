HR Data Analyst

About the role
Join the People team to turn workforce data into clear reporting for leaders across the business.

What you will do
- Own monthly headcount, attrition and hiring dashboards
- Answer ad-hoc questions from HR business partners with SQL and Excel
- Improve data quality in the HRMS and document metric definitions
- Present findings to managers in plain language

Requirements
- 1+ years of experience in data or HR analytics
- Strong SQL and Excel
- Dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
- Data cleaning and data visualization
- Clear written and verbal communication

Nice to have
- Python (Pandas) for automation
- Statistics fundamentals
- Experience with HR analytics or people analytics
