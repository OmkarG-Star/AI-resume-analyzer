Data Scientist (Mid-level) — People Analytics

About the role
We are building our people analytics function and need a Data Scientist who can turn HR and workforce data into models that managers actually use.

What you will do
- Build and deploy machine learning models for attrition risk, hiring funnel forecasting and workforce planning
- Own data preparation in Python and SQL, from raw HRMS extracts to clean feature tables
- Explain model outputs to HR business partners with clear dashboards and written insights
- Work with engineering to put models behind APIs and monitor them in production

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in data science or analytics
- Strong Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn) and SQL
- Hands-on machine learning: classification, regression, feature engineering and model evaluation
- Experience building dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
- Statistics and A/B testing fundamentals
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field

Nice to have
- Explainable AI (SHAP) and time series forecasting
- FastAPI or Flask, Docker and Git
- Experience with HR analytics or people analytics
- Exposure to AWS or Azure
